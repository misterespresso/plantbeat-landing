PlantBeat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026 (applies to PlantBeat 3.0 and later)

PlantBeat is made by Caffeinated Software LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: administrator@plantbeat.com.

The short version

  • PlantBeat has no accounts. We do not ask for your name or email.
  • Your plants, photos, notes and care history are stored on your phone. We do not have a copy.
  • If you use an AI feature (identify, diagnose, care guide, Sage chat), the photo or text you send goes to our server and then to an AI company to get you an answer. The app asks for your permission before the first time. Details below.
  • We do not run ads, do not track you across apps or websites, and do not sell data.

What stays on your phone

Your plants, care history, schedules, notes, photos, settings and Sage chat history are saved on your device only. Care reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Removing the app removes this data. The export feature creates a file that you choose where to save or share; we never receive it. Because there is no cloud backup, an export file is your only backup.

What we collect and why

  1. A random device ID. On first launch the app creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple or Google account. We use it to count your monthly AI uses, to limit abuse, and to check whether your device has a Pro subscription. A new ID is created if you reinstall the app.
  2. Photos you send for identify or diagnose. The app resizes the photo and sends it to our server, which passes it to Google Gemini to get the answer. We do not store the photo or write it in our logs.
  3. Text you send to Sage, the plant chat. Your messages, and the name, care profile and recent care history of the plant you are asking about, go to our server, which passes them to GreenPT, an AI provider based in the EU, to write the reply. We do not store the messages or write them in our logs.
  4. Care guide requests. To create an AI care guide for a plant that is not in the built-in database, the plant name is sent to our server and on to Google Gemini. The resulting guide is cached on our server for up to 90 days under the plant name only. It is not linked to you.
  5. Purchase information. If you buy PlantBeat Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm that your device has an active subscription. RevenueCat receives the random device ID and the purchase information from the store.
  6. Crash reports. In some builds, crash and error reports go to Sentry. They contain technical details about the device (such as the model, operating system and app version) and the error. They do not contain your name, email, plants, photos or messages, and we attach no user ID to them.
  7. Server logs. Our server writes short log lines for each request: the route, a short part of the device ID, the result, how long it took and the estimated cost. They contain no photos or message text.

Third-party AI services (AI disclosure)

When you use an AI feature, the data you chose to send is shared with these companies, who process it on our behalf to produce the answer. The app asks for your permission the first time, and you can withdraw it in Settings → "AI and your data".

FeatureData sentCompany
Identify a plantThe photo you took or pickedGoogle (Gemini API)
Diagnose a problemThe photo, and for a saved plant its name, care profile and recent care historyGoogle (Gemini API)
AI care guideThe plant nameGoogle (Gemini API)
Sage plant chatYour messages and the plant's name, care profile and recent careGreenPT (EU)

These features send data only when you use them, and need an internet connection. If you do not want your photo or message to go to these services, do not use these features: the rest of the app works without them and without internet. Google's and GreenPT's own terms and privacy policies also apply to their processing. AI answers can be wrong; do not rely on them for the health of people or pets.

Who else receives data

  • Google (Gemini API) and GreenPT: AI processing, as above.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our server and its short-lived counters and logs.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google: subscriptions.
  • Sentry: crash reports.

We share data with these companies only to run the app. We do not sell or rent data and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep data

  • Photos and chat text: not kept by us.
  • Monthly AI usage counters tied to the device ID: kept until the month ends plus about a week.
  • Server logs: kept for the period Cloudflare keeps them on our plan.
  • Cached AI care guides: up to 90 days, not tied to you.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their policies.
  • Retention at Google and GreenPT is set by their terms.

Your choices and rights

You can delete everything the app stores by deleting the app. We hold no account, so we cannot look you up by name. If you want data tied to your device ID or purchases deleted, or have a question about this policy, email administrator@plantbeat.com and say when you used the app. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data; we will answer such requests within 30 days, as far as we are able to link them to you.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: only to take or choose a plant photo.
  • Notifications: for care reminders.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts or microphone.

Children

PlantBeat is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Earlier versions

PlantBeat versions before 3.0 used optional accounts stored with Supabase. That service is being shut down; data from those versions is kept only in an offline backup and is deleted on request (email above).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for a significant change, say so in the app update notes.
