PlantBeat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

This privacy policy is applicable to the PlantBeat app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by Caffeinated Software LLC (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".

The Service Provider acts as the data controller for your personal information under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

User Provided Information

The Application creates a guest account when you first open it so core features work immediately. Registration is not required, but some features are only available with a registered account.

When you create an account or use the Application, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and display name
  • Plant Care Information: Your plant collection, care schedules, watering logs, care history, and plant notes
  • AI Chat Conversations: Messages and images you send to Sage, our AI plant assistant, including conversation history
  • Community Content: Posts, comments, images, and interactions you share in our plant identification and diagnosis communities
  • User Preferences: Plant care settings, notification preferences, and app customization choices
  • Care Photos: Images you upload for plant identification, diagnosis, or to save in your plant collection. These images are stored in our cloud storage to power app features.
  • Support Communications: Information you provide when contacting us for support

Guest Accounts

When you open PlantBeat for the first time, a guest account is automatically created. This allows you to try features like plant identification and AI chat before signing up. Guest account data (plants, care logs, photos, chat history) is stored on our servers and is linked to your device. After 14 days, you will be required to create a full account to continue using the Application. If you create an account, your guest data is preserved. If you do not create an account, all guest data is permanently deleted 30 days after account creation.

Automatically Collected Information

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to:

  • The type of mobile device you use
  • Your mobile device's unique device ID
  • The IP address of your mobile device
  • Your mobile operating system
  • The type of mobile Internet browsers you use
  • Information about the way you use the Application
  • Usage analytics and interaction patterns
  • App Events: Interaction events such as screen views, onboarding progress, and feature usage. Plant search queries are recorded to help us identify missing species and expand our plant database.

Camera and Photo Access

The Application may access your device's camera to capture photos of plants for identification and diagnosis purposes. When you use our AI identification features, images are sent to our AI services for processing.

Premium Subscription Data

If you subscribe to premium features, we process subscription status and payment information through third-party payment processors (Apple App Store or Google Play Store). We do not store your complete payment information directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Service Delivery

  • Plant identification and diagnosis using AI services (Kindwise and Google Gemini)
  • AI-powered plant care chat assistant (Sage)
  • Managing your plant collection and care schedules
  • Providing care reminders and push notifications
  • Enabling community features and interactions

Service Improvement

  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve the Application
  • Debugging and troubleshooting issues
  • Retaining images sent to AI services and community features for service quality improvement. This does not include photos saved to your personal plant collection.

Communication

  • Sending service notifications and updates
  • Responding to support requests
  • Providing important information about your account

3. Location Information

The Application does not request precise device location (GPS). However, our analytics providers may infer your approximate location (country, region, city, or timezone) from your IP address and device metadata. This approximate location data may be used to:

  • Analyze regional usage patterns to improve the Application
  • Provide region-appropriate plant care context in AI responses
  • Identify trends and improve overall service performance

If we add features that require precise device location in the future (such as weather integration), we will request your explicit permission through your device's location services prompt before collecting any GPS data.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

The Application shares data with third-party service providers to deliver its features. This includes sending plant photos to AI services for identification and diagnosis, storing your data in our cloud infrastructure, and transmitting usage analytics to our analytics provider. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

The Application utilizes third-party services that have their own Privacy Policy about handling data. Our data processing partners include:

  • Kindwise (Czech Republic/EU) — Plant identification AI service. Receives plant photos and returns identification results.
    Kindwise Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini AI (United States) — Plant identification, diagnosis, and AI chat service. Receives text messages and plant photos.
    Google Gemini Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (United States) — Database, authentication, and cloud storage. Stores all user data including accounts, plant data, photos, and chat history.
    Supabase Privacy Policy
  • PostHog (United States) — Product analytics and event tracking. Receives usage events and device info.
    PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (for Android users)
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (for iOS users) — App distribution and in-app purchases
    Apple Privacy Policy

All third-party processors maintain appropriate data protection agreements and comply with GDPR and CCPA requirements.

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process
  • When they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request
  • With their trusted service providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement

5. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in:

  • United States: Supabase (infrastructure), Google Gemini (AI processing), PostHog (analytics)
  • European Union: Kindwise (AI processing, Czech Republic)

For transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and other appropriate safeguards to ensure adequate protection of your data.

6. Data Retention

The Service Provider will retain your data as follows:

  • Guest Accounts: Guest account data is permanently deleted 30 days after account creation if no full account is created
  • Active Accounts: User data is retained for as long as your account remains active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data is removed within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Plant Care Data: Your plant care logs, schedules, and collection information will be retained for as long as your account is active
  • AI and Community Images: Images sent to AI services for identification or diagnosis, and images shared in community features, may be retained for service improvement. Photos saved to your personal plant collection are not subject to this retention policy and remain under your control.
  • Community Content: Posts and comments in community features may be retained to maintain the integrity of community discussions, even after account deletion, but will be anonymized
  • Automatically Collected Information: Retained for up to 24 months and thereafter may be stored in aggregate

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing of your personal data
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at administrator@plantbeat.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

EU Residents

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR and may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request deletion.

8. Opt-Out Rights

You can halt all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling the Application. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

You can also:

  • Deny camera permissions to prevent photo-based plant identification features
  • Opt out of community features by not posting or participating in discussions
  • Cancel premium subscriptions through your app store account settings
  • Disable push notifications in your device settings
  • Contact us at administrator@plantbeat.com to opt out of analytics tracking

9. Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data. To delete your account:

  1. Contact us at administrator@plantbeat.com with your deletion request
  2. We will confirm your identity and process the request within 30 days
  3. Some data may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes as outlined in this policy

10. Children's Privacy

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

  • The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13
  • Users between ages 13–17 require parental or guardian consent
  • The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age
  • In the case the Service Provider discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers
  • If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider (administrator@plantbeat.com) so that they will be able to take the necessary actions

11. AI and Automated Processing

The Application uses AI services to provide plant identification and care suggestions. These suggestions are advisory only and are provided for educational and informational purposes. No automated decisions are made that legally or significantly affect you based on AI processing alone.

12. Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information we process and maintain:

  • All data transmission between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS encryption
  • Plant care data and user information are stored securely using our third-party database provider's security measures
  • Access to personal information is limited to authorized employees and contractors who need to know that information in order to operate, develop, or improve the Application

Please be aware that, although we endeavor to provide reasonable security for information we process and maintain, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by:

  • Updating this page with the new Privacy Policy
  • Sending in-app notifications for significant changes
  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy

You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

14. Your Consent

By using the Application, you are giving your consent to the Service Provider processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by us. "Processing" means using cookies on a computer/hand held device or using or touching information in any way, including, but not limited to, collecting, storing, deleting, using, combining and disclosing information.

15. Contact Us
